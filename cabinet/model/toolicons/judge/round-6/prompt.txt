icon enlarged so you can see it. Each icon is numbered and labelled with its tool; below is what each
tool does. Judge how well each icon represents its tool.

For every numbered icon, score each rubric category 0-10 with the evidence you actually see in the
picture, and a concrete fix (what to change in the 3D object: add/remove/move/recolour a part). Empty

Be strict. 9 means a modeller would get it instantly and nothing needs changing; 7 means it works but
something is weak; 5 or less means wrong or unreadable. Do not be generous to be nice.

Rubric:
- represents (weight 3): The icon shows what this tool does: its own idea (object or action), so a modeller seeing it next to the name gets it instantly. Not a generic shape that could be any tool.
- distinct (weight 2): It can't be confused with any other icon in the same category (shape, colour and action differ).
- legibility (weight 2): At wheel size (the 1x row) the silhouette and the key part read clearly: nothing important is a lone pixel, thin parts survive, the red accent marks the action.
- craft (weight 1): Clean pixel art in the chest's style: solid outline, no muddy dithering, no stray bits, well framed.
