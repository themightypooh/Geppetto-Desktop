# Adult rice weevil (*Sitophilus oryzae*) — reference record

The sculpt is an original Effigy construction, enlarged for display. The two reference photographs are displayed beside the model, not baked into its material or exported mesh. Both local copies have their EXIF orientation applied; no other edits.

The exterior is a species-informed likeness, not a specimen diagnosis: rice and maize weevils can require genital characters to separate reliably. No underside photograph was approved, so concealed morphology is conservative rather than presented as verified.
